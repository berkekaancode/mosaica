# 11 — Future AI Capability Architecture

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the future role of Artificial Intelligence capabilities within Mosaica.

AI is considered an optional future enhancement and is not required for the initial Mosaica product.

The core value of Mosaica is the personal cultural archive experience.

Future AI capabilities may enhance this experience without becoming a dependency of the core system.



# AI Position in Mosaica

AI is not part of the initial product foundation.

The initial Mosaica system shall operate completely without AI dependencies.

# Initial Product Scope

The first version of Mosaica focuses on:

- Content management,

- Library management,

- Personal organization,

- Personal notes,

- Cultural archive creation.

AI features are excluded from the initial implementation scope.



# Future AI Possibilities

Future versions may introduce:

- personalized recommendations,

- archive analysis,

- intelligent search,

- cultural pattern discovery,

- AI-assisted organization.

These capabilities require separate evaluation before implementation.



# AI Principles

Future AI features shall follow these principles:

## Principle 1 — AI Supports Users

AI provides suggestions and assistance.

Users remain responsible for decisions.



## Principle 2 — AI Does Not Replace Core Logic

AI shall not contain:

- business rules,

- ownership rules,

- core domain decisions.



## Principle 3 — AI Respects Privacy

AI capabilities must follow Mosaica security and data ownership principles.



## Principle 4 — AI Requires Justification

AI features shall only be introduced when they provide meaningful User value.
